How H3X Search works

H3X Search searches Xianyu (Goofish) listings for you and shows them in English or Portuguese, with prices in Brazilian reais. You don't need a Chinese account or phone number. When you find something, the Buy on Hubbuy button opens the listing on Hubbuy, a purchasing agent that buys from the seller and ships internationally.

Updated 13 November 2026

Why the site exists

Xianyu is one of China's largest second-hand marketplaces, but it isn't built for people outside China. The app normally requires a Chinese account, and Xianyu's web search stopped returning results for visitors from Brazil. The H3xCN community built H3X Search to fix the first problem: finding the listing.

What happens when you search

  1. You type a term. By default it is sent exactly as typed. With Translate search to Chinese on, everyday words are translated first.
  2. The site queries Xianyu listings and translates the titles into the page language.
  3. You get results, 20 per page, with more pages when available.
  4. To buy, you click Buy on Hubbuy. It is the only action on each listing.

What each listing shows

  • the listing photo, loaded straight from the marketplace's image servers;
  • the title, translated into English (or Portuguese);
  • the price in reais at the day's rate, plus the original price in yuan (CNY);
  • the seller's region in China;
  • how many people marked that they “want” it, a sign of demand;
  • the Buy on Hubbuy button.

Literal or translated search

Chinese sellers write models and codes in Latin letters and numbers: rtx 3060, ddr5 6800mhz, sony a6400. So the search is literal by default. Words like sneakers won't appear in a Chinese listing; for those, turn translation on or type the Chinese term. See Search tips for examples and a term list.

Prices in reais are estimates

The reais figure is the listing price converted at the day's yuan rate. It helps you compare; it is not the total cost, which also includes shipping inside China (if any), agent fees, international shipping and import taxes.

What H3X Search does not do

  • it doesn't sell products or take payments;
  • it has no login, accounts or profiles;
  • it can't vouch for a listing, seller or item condition;
  • it is not affiliated with Xianyu, Goofish or Alibaba.
