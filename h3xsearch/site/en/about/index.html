About H3X Search

H3X Search is made by H3xCN, a Brazilian community on Discord about buying from China. The site is free, independent and sells nothing. Buy links go to Hubbuy with H3xCN's invite code.

Updated 13 November 2026

Why we built it

The community uses Xianyu to find second-hand and hard-to-find items. When Xianyu's web search stopped returning results for visitors from Brazil, it became hard even to check whether something existed there. H3X Search brings that search back, translated, with prices in reais and a direct way to buy.

What the site is, and isn't

  • It is a search tool that shows Xianyu listings with translated titles and converted prices.
  • It is not a shop: no sales, no payments, no stock.
  • No accounts, logins or profiles.
  • Not affiliated with or endorsed by Xianyu, Goofish or Alibaba.
  • Prices in reais are estimates at the day's rate.

Disclosure: the Hubbuy link

Each listing has one button, Buy on Hubbuy, which opens the item on Hubbuy, a purchasing agent that buys from the Chinese seller and ships internationally. These links, and the sign-up link, carry H3xCN's invite code: a referral link (Hubbuy's ambassador programme), so the community may benefit when someone signs up or buys through it.

H3X Search charges nothing and adds no fee to what you pay at Hubbuy. Prices, shipping, timing and support for the purchase are Hubbuy's. You don't have to use the referral link.
