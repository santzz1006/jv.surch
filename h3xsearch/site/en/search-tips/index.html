How to search Xianyu well

Three rules cover most searches: for models and codes, type them exactly with translation off (rtx 3060, ddr5 6800mhz); for everyday words, turn on “Translate search to Chinese” or type the Chinese term; and use few words.

Updated 13 November 2026

Literal search: models, codes, brands

Sellers write the model as printed on the box. Searching without translation sends your term unchanged, so it matches the listing title.

  • graphics cards: rtx 3060, rx 6700 xt
  • memory: ddr5 6800mhz
  • cameras: sony a6400, fuji x100v
  • consoles: switch oled, ps5

Many brands work in Latin letters (nike, sony); some are more common in Chinese: 耐克 (Nike), 索尼 (Sony), 任天堂 (Nintendo), 北面 (The North Face).

Translated search: everyday words

Sneakers or jacket won't appear in a Chinese listing. Turn on Translate search to Chinese under the search box; the results header shows the Chinese term that was used. If results look off, type the Chinese term yourself from the tables below.

Fewer words, more listings

Every extra word must appear in the listing title. Start short and narrow down.
